Video messaging has become a standard part of sales outreach. A short, personalized video can cut through the noise much better than a string of text emails. But which tool should you pick? Loom, Vidyard, and BombBomb are the three most popular options, but they serve slightly different purposes.

Loom is the go-to for quick asynchronous videos. Vidyard leans into advanced analytics and enterprise sales. BombBomb focuses on relationship building with integrated email video. I’ve spent the last few weeks testing all three in real sales workflows. Here’s how they stack up.

What Each Tool Does Best

Before we get into the fine print, here’s a one-sentence summary:

  • Loom = fastest way to record and share a video (screen + face) with zero friction.
  • Vidyard = full-featured video platform with deep sales analytics, CRM integrations, and team management.
  • BombBomb = video embedded directly in email, designed for one-to-one relationship sales (real estate, insurance, coaching).

Now let’s look at each one in detail.

Loom – The Speed Champion

Loom’s claim to fame is simplicity. You install the browser extension or desktop app, click record, and get a link instantly. No editing, no hosting, no fuss. For sales reps who need to record a quick walkthrough or personalized demo, Loom is hard to beat.

Key features:

  • Unlimited video length on paid plans (free plan caps at 5 minutes).
  • Viewer analytics: who watched, for how long, and whether they rewatched.
  • Calls-to-action (CTA) buttons you can add to videos.
  • Transcripts and captions automatically generated.
  • Embeddable player (works on email, Slack, CRM via link).

Pricing (as of 2026):

  • Free: Limited to 25 videos, 5 min max, basic analytics.
  • Business: $15/month per creator (annual) – unlimited videos, custom branding, advanced analytics, priority support.
  • Enterprise: Custom – SSO, audit logs, API access.

Best for: Quick, informal videos where you just need to get a message across. Think sales prospecting, team updates, or product feedback.

Pros:

  • Ridiculously easy to start recording.
  • Works everywhere – browser, desktop, mobile.
  • Free tier is generous enough for light use.
  • Great for internal communication (async standups, bug reports).

Cons:

  • No native video editing (can only trim front/back).
  • Viewer analytics are basic compared to Vidyard.
  • No option to embed video directly in email body (uses link only).
  • Can feel too casual for high-stakes client outreach.

Vidyard – The Sales Analytics Powerhouse

Vidyard positions itself as a sales enablement platform, not just a video recorder. If you’re running a sales team and want to track exactly how prospects engage with your videos, Vidyard gives you the deepest data.

Key features:

  • Individual viewer tracking: see name, company, time watched per section.
  • CRM integrations (Salesforce, Hubspot, Pipedrive) that log video views automatically.
  • Video hosting and library management for teams.
  • Custom thumbnails, CTAs, forms, and A/B testing.
  • Screen recording + Webcam, with optional editing.
  • AI-powered transcription and highlights.

Pricing:

  • Free: Unlimited videos, 30 min max per video, basic analytics (no viewer names).
  • Pro: $19/month per user (annual) – CRM integrations, custom CTAs, team workspaces.
  • Enterprise: Custom – unlimited users, advanced security, dedicated support.

Best for: Sales teams that want to tie video engagement directly to CRM records. Also good for creating a library of reusable product demo videos.

Pros:

  • Unmatched analytics – you know exactly who watched and what they rewatched.
  • Native CRM integration means less manual data entry.
  • Customizable video players (branding, colors).
  • Mobile-friendly hosting and sharing.

Cons:

  • Learning curve is steeper than Loom.
  • Free plan hides viewer identities – you need Pro to see names.
  • Recording process feels clunkier (takes a few extra clicks).
  • Can be overkill for a solo salesperson.

BombBomb – Video Inside Email, No Link Required

BombBomb takes a completely different approach: it records and embeds the video directly into the email body (as a GIF preview that plays when clicked). This is huge for industries where recipients may not click an external link (real estate agents, financial advisors, etc.). It’s built for one-to-one relationship selling.

Key features:

  • Video embedded inside email (GIF preview with a “play” button).
  • Record from webcam or screen share (no mobile app for recording, but you can send via mobile).
  • Track opens, clicks, and video plays.
  • Templates and email scheduling.
  • Integrates with major CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho).
  • Custom branding and team management.

Pricing:

  • Starter: $33/month per user (billed annually) – 50 video uploads, basic tracking.
  • Plus: $50/month per user (annual) – unlimited videos, custom branding, advanced analytics.
  • Enterprise: Custom – API, SSO, priority support.
  • Also offers a 14-day free trial.

Best for: Salespeople who rely heavily on email outreach and want the video to appear seamlessly (no link, no extra clicks). Common in real estate, insurance, coaching, and high-touch services.

Pros:

  • Video plays inside email without forcing a new tab – better engagement.
  • GIF thumbnail auto-generates, so recipients see a preview.
  • Good tracking that ties back to email opens.
  • Feels more personal and less “mass-market” than Loom.

Cons:

  • Expensive for a single user.
  • No dedicated desktop app (only browser extension + web app).
  • Screen recording is limited (no full editor).
  • Mobile recording is clunky (only via browser, no app).
  • Less suitable for internal team videos.

Detailed Breakdown: Where Each Tool Shines (and Falls Short)

Ease of Use

Loom is the clear winner here. You click one button, the recording starts, and you’re done. Vidyard requires setting up your account, choosing recording settings, and often fiddling with integrations before you get going. BombBomb is somewhere in the middle – its browser extension works fine, but the lack of a mobile app hurts if you’re on the go.

Analytics Depth

Vidyard dominates. You can see not only who watched, but what part they rewatched, whether they shared it, and how they interacted with CTAs. Loom only shows total views and watch time (unless you pay for Enterprise). BombBomb gives you email-level data (opens, plays) but doesn’t break down video sections.

Email Integration

BombBomb is the only tool that embeds a playable video inside the email body (as a GIF preview). This is huge for open rates – studies show an increase of 30-50% compared to link-only videos. Loom and Vidyard rely on you copying a link, which can get buried or dismissed.

Team Collaboration

If you have a sales team, Vidyard’s workspace features are the best. You can create a shared library of demo videos, assign videos to reps, and track performance across the team. Loom’s team features are simpler (shared folders, basic analytics per user). BombBomb’s team management is adequate but lacks the library sharing.

Price for Solo Users

For a single salesperson on a budget, Loom’s $15/month plan is the cheapest. Vidyard’s Pro at $19 is still affordable but you get more features. BombBomb is significantly more expensive at $50/month for unlimited videos, making it hard to justify unless you really need the in-email video.

Real-World Use Cases

Scenario 1: Outbound cold emails

If you’re sending 50+ personalized videos per week, bombing each with a link via Loom or Vidyard works fine. But BombBomb’s in-email video will likely get more plays. The trade-off is cost and ease of recording.

Scenario 2: Existing customer demos

For existing client relationships, Loom’s quick recording is perfect. A 2-minute screen share showing how to use a feature feels natural and not overly produced.

Scenario 3: Sales team pipeline tracking

Vidyard is the best choice if you use Salesforce or HubSpot and want every video view to appear as an activity inside the CRM. It reduces manual logging and helps managers see which deals are warm.

Scenario 4: Real estate / insurance / high-trust industries

BombBomb’s personal touch (seeing a familiar face inside the email) builds trust faster than a link. Many agents use it for property tours or client updates.

Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Pick Loom if: You’re a solo salesperson or small team that wants the simplest possible video tool. You don’t need deep analytics and you send links in emails or Slack. Loom is also great for internal use (standups, feedback). It’s the best value for most small businesses.

Pick Vidyard if: You have a sales team of 3+ people and you’re serious about tracking video engagement. The CRM integration alone can save hours per week. Vidyard is the tool to scale with.

Pick BombBomb if: Your primary channel is email and you want to maximize open and click rates. If you sell to industries like real estate, insurance, or coaching where clients are less likely to click a link, BombBomb’s embedded video gives you an edge. Just be ready to pay more per seat.

For overall balance of cost, features, and ease: Loom wins for most small businesses. Vidyard wins for data-driven teams. BombBomb wins for email-centric relationship selling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Loom for free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 25 videos, 5 minutes max each, and basic analytics. Enough to test it out.

Does Vidyard allow screen recording with face overlay?

Yes, both Loom and Vidyard support screen + webcam recording. BombBomb also supports this but only via browser.

Which tool integrates with HubSpot?

All three integrate with HubSpot. Vidyard’s integration is the most thorough (activity logging, contact enrichment). Loom has a basic Zapier connection. BombBomb offers native HubSpot integration.

Is BombBomb worth the higher price?

It depends on your industry. If you send hundreds of personalized emails every week and get better results from in-email video, then yes. If you’re mostly sharing links, spend less on Loom or Vidyard.

Can I use these tools for internal team videos?

Loom is the best for internal use – quick, informal, and widely used. Vidyard can work but feels more formal. BombBomb is not designed for internal videos.

Do any of these offer video editing beyond trimming?

Not really. All three are focused on quick recording and sharing. If you need editing, consider tools like Screenflow or Camtasia, then upload to your chosen video platform.

Which one has the best mobile app?

Loom has a well-built mobile app for both iOS and Android. Vidyard also has a mobile app. BombBomb does not have a dedicated mobile recording app – you can only record via browser, which is inconvenient.

Bottom Line

There’s no single “best” video messaging tool for sales. Your choice comes down to how you communicate with prospects.

  • Loom is the easiest and most affordable.
  • Vidyard is the most analytical and team-friendly.
  • BombBomb is the most email-native and personal.

Try the free versions of each (Loom and Vidyard have generous free tiers; BombBomb offers a 14-day trial). That’s the best way to see which one fits your workflow.

If I had to recommend one tool for a small business owner just starting with video outreach, it would be Loom. It’s fast, cheap, and effective. As you grow and need deeper data, Vidyard scales well. And if you’re in a trust-heavy industry, BombBomb is worth the premium.
