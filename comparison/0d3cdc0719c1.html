Comparison Overview

Freelancers need accounting software that does three things well: invoicing, expense tracking, and tax prep. But the devil is in the details – how easy is it to create a recurring invoice? Can you snap a receipt photo from your phone? Does it handle multiple currencies? And most importantly – does the price fit your budget?

QuickBooks, Xero, and FreshBooks are the three most popular choices for self-employed professionals. Each has strengths and weaknesses. This comparison breaks down everything you need to know – features, pricing, integrations, and real-world use cases – so you can pick the right tool without wasting time or money.

What is QuickBooks Online?

QuickBooks Online (QBO) is the most widely used small business accounting software in the US. It’s a cloud-based platform from Intuit that handles everything from invoicing and expense tracking to inventory management and payroll.

Key features:

  • Automated bank feeds and transaction categorization
  • Project profitability tracking (see profit per client/project)
  • 1099 contractor management (file directly from QBO)
  • Inventory tracking (for product-based freelancers)
  • Robust reporting: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, sales tax
  • Payroll integration (via QuickBooks Payroll, separate fee)
  • Mobile app with receipt capture

Pricing: Simple Start ($30/mo), Essentials ($55/mo), Plus ($85/mo), Advanced ($200/mo).

Free trial: 30 days.

Who it’s for: Freelancers who need serious reporting, manage subcontractors, or sell physical products. It’s overkill if you only send a few invoices a month.

What is Xero?

Xero is a New Zealand-based accounting platform that’s gained massive traction in the US and UK. It’s known for its clean design, unlimited users (even on the cheapest plan), and strong bank reconciliation tools.

Key features:

  • Unlimited bank feeds and transaction matching
  • Unlimited users (all plans)
  • Pay bills and purchase orders
  • Project tracking (with time & costs)
  • Inventory (basic, on higher plans)
  • 800+ integrations via its marketplace
  • HubDoc integration for automatic bill capture
  • Multi-currency support on all plans

Pricing: Starter ($13/mo), Standard ($37/mo), Premium ($55/mo).

Free trial: 30 days.

Who it’s for: Freelancers with multiple bank accounts, teams (even a two-person shop), or those who want a modern interface without the QuickBooks learning curve. Xero shines when you need to collaborate with an accountant or bookkeeper – they get unlimited access for free.

What is FreshBooks?

FreshBooks is built from the ground up for service-based freelancers. It started as an invoicing tool and grew into a full accounting platform. Its tagline – “Accounting software for small business owners who aren’t accountants” – says it all.

Key features:

  • Beautiful, customizable invoices (with payment links)
  • Time tracking (with billable rates per project)
  • Expense tracking (auto-import from bank feeds)
  • Project management (tasks, milestones, team collaboration)
  • Client portal (share estimates, invoices, and files)
  • Proposals and estimates (convert to invoices with one click)
  • Late payment reminders and recurring invoices
  • Mobile app with receipt scanning

Pricing: Lite ($17/mo), Plus ($30/mo), Premium ($55/mo).

Free trial: 30 days.

Who it’s for: Solo freelancers, consultants, designers, writers, and anyone who bills by the hour or project. If you hate spreadsheets and want to spend less time on accounting, FreshBooks is your best bet.

Hidden costs to watch:

  • QuickBooks charges extra for payroll ($45+/mo) and for each additional user ($10/user/mo for Simple Start, $15/user/mo for Essentials).
  • Xero’s Starter plan limits invoices to 20 per month and bills to 5 per month. You’ll likely need Standard ($37/mo) if you send more than 20 invoices.
  • FreshBooks’ Lite plan limits you to 5 billable clients. If you have more, you’ll need Plus ($30/mo) or Premium ($55/mo).

Pros and Cons of QuickBooks Online

Pros:

  • Most comprehensive reporting (project profitability, job costing, sales tax)
  • Excellent 1099 contractor management – file directly from the platform
  • Inventory tracking for product sellers
  • Strong payroll integration (QuickBooks Payroll)
  • Huge ecosystem of apps and integrations, including Zapier for workflow automation

Cons:

  • Steep learning curve – not intuitive for non-accountants
  • Expensive once you add payroll, users, or advanced features
  • Bank feed transaction limits on lower plans (250/mo on Simple Start)
  • Customer support can be slow and scripted
  • Interface feels cluttered compared to Xero or FreshBooks

Pros and Cons of Xero

Pros:

  • Unlimited users on all plans – great for freelancers with a bookkeeper
  • Unlimited bank feeds and transaction matching (no caps)
  • Clean, modern interface – easier to navigate than QuickBooks
  • Strong multi-currency support (all plans)
  • 800+ integrations, including HubSpot and Stripe

Cons:

  • Starter plan is very limited (20 invoices, 5 bills per month)
  • Inventory is basic and only on Standard plan
  • No built-in payroll for US users (requires third-party app like Gusto)
  • Customer support is email/chat only on lower plans
  • Learning curve for advanced features like report customization

Pros and Cons of FreshBooks

Pros:

  • Easiest to use – setup in minutes, clean interface
  • Best invoicing templates – clients love the look and payment flow
  • Built-in time tracking with billable rates – perfect for hourly freelancers
  • Great client portal (share estimates, invoices, files)
  • Responsive customer support (phone, email, chat on all plans)

Cons:

  • No inventory management – not suitable for product sellers
  • Client limits on lower plans (5 billable clients on Lite)
  • Limited reporting compared to QuickBooks (no balance sheet or P&L on Lite)
  • Integrations library is smaller (100+ vs 800+ for Xero)
  • Multi-currency only on Plus plan

Who Should Choose QuickBooks Online?

  • You sell physical products – inventory tracking is essential.
  • You manage multiple 1099 contractors – QuickBooks makes filing easy.
  • You need deep project profitability reports – see exactly which clients are most profitable.
  • You have an accountant who uses QuickBooks – collaboration is smooth.
  • You’re willing to invest time learning the software – the power is there, but it’s not plug-and-play.

Avoid if: You hate complexity, only send a few invoices a month, or don’t need inventory/contractor features.

Who Should Choose Xero?

  • You have multiple bank accounts or high transaction volume – unlimited feeds and reconciliation.
  • You want to give your accountant/team free access – unlimited users included.
  • You work with international clients – multi-currency on all plans.
  • You prefer a modern, clean interface – Xero is the most visually polished.
  • You need strong integrations – 800+ apps, including ConvertKit for email marketing automation.

Avoid if: You send more than 20 invoices a month on a budget (Starter plan is too limited), or you want an all-in-one solution with payroll.

Who Should Choose FreshBooks?

  • You’re a solo freelancer (writer, designer, consultant, coach) – simple, fast, and client-friendly.
  • You bill by the hour or project – time tracking and invoicing are tightly integrated.
  • You want to look professional – FreshBooks invoices are the best-looking in the category.
  • You hate accounting and want to spend as little time on it as possible – the learning curve is nearly zero.
  • You value customer support – you can actually get a human on the phone quickly.

Avoid if: You need inventory, manage a team (user limits), or require advanced reporting like balance sheets on the cheapest plan.

Final Verdict

After comparing features, pricing, and real-world usability, here’s the bottom line:

For most freelancers, FreshBooks is the best choice. It’s the easiest to use, has the best invoicing and time tracking, and offers excellent customer support. The Lite plan at $17/mo is affordable for solo pros, and the Plus plan at $30/mo handles up to 50 billable clients – plenty for a growing freelance business.

QuickBooks Online is the best choice if you need inventory, 1099 contractor management, or advanced project profitability reporting. But be prepared for a steeper learning curve and higher costs as you add features.

Xero is the best choice if you want unlimited users, unlimited bank feeds, and a modern interface. It’s especially strong for freelancers who work with an accountant or have multiple bank accounts.

My pick: FreshBooks Plus ($30/mo) – it’s the sweet spot for freelancers. And to automate your invoicing workflow (e.g., send an invoice when a project is marked complete in your project management tool), connect FreshBooks with Zapier – it’s free to start and saves hours every month.

FAQ

Is QuickBooks or Xero better for freelancers?

It depends on your needs. QuickBooks is better for freelancers who need inventory, contractor management, or detailed project profitability. Xero is better for freelancers who want unlimited users, unlimited bank feeds, and a cleaner interface. For most solo freelancers, FreshBooks is actually the best option.

Can I use FreshBooks for free?

FreshBooks offers a 30-day free trial (no credit card required). After that, plans start at $17/mo for Lite (up to 5 billable clients). There is no permanent free tier.

Does Xero have a mobile app?

Yes, Xero has mobile apps for iOS and Android. You can create invoices, capture receipts, reconcile transactions, and view financial reports on the go.

Which accounting software integrates with Stripe?

All three integrate with Stripe. QuickBooks, Xero, and FreshBooks all allow you to accept credit card payments via Stripe directly from invoices. FreshBooks also integrates with Stripe for payment processing, which is a popular choice among freelancers.

How do I automate invoicing with these tools?

You can use Zapier to connect your accounting software with other apps. For example, create a Zap that automatically generates a FreshBooks invoice when a new client is added in HubSpot, or send a payment reminder via ConvertKit when an invoice is overdue. All three tools have Zapier integrations.

Which is the cheapest accounting software for freelancers?

Xero’s Starter plan at $13/mo is the cheapest entry price, but it’s limited to 20 invoices and 5 bills per month. FreshBooks Lite at $17/mo gives you unlimited invoices
