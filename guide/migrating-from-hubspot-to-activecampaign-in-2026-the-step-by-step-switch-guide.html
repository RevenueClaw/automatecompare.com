Before You Switch: Prep Checklist

Export everything before you touch anything: contacts with custom fields and tags (HubSpot exports CSV from Contacts), deals and pipelines (if you use Sales Hub), lists, templates, and every workflow - screenshot the automation logic because no import carries it over. Note your current plan renewal date: HubSpot runs on subscriptions with no promo pricing, so there is no discount left on the table - you are free to leave whenever.

Map your HubSpot properties to ActiveCampaign fields before import. ActiveCampaign's Starter plan ($15/mo for 1,000 contacts, billed yearly) prices by contact count, and HubSpot Starter is $20/mo flat for the same list - both scale with your database, so clean out unsubscribes and dead contacts first. Your bill follows your contact count on both platforms.

The Step-by-Step Migration

1. Export from HubSpot. Contacts > Export (CSV, include list memberships and lifecycle stage). Export deals separately if you use the CRM side.

2. Clean the data. Dedupe emails, merge tags into a simple tag scheme - ActiveCampaign tags power its automation builder, so a messy tag import will haunt every workflow you build.

3. Import into ActiveCampaign. Settings > Import > Contacts. Map HubSpot properties to ActiveCampaign fields (email, first name, last name, custom fields), and import your tags as ActiveCampaign tags.

4. Rebuild automations. This is the part that takes real work. HubSpot workflows and ActiveCampaign automations are both visual builders, but conditions and actions do not map 1:1. Rebuild your top three automations first (welcome sequence, lead nurture, re-engagement), test with a seed contact, then rebuild the long tail.

5. Recreate forms and landing pages. Embed new ActiveCampaign forms wherever HubSpot forms were live; kill the old HubSpot embeds only after the new ones test clean.

6. Warm up your sending. ActiveCampaign's shared sending reputation is solid, but a sudden list-wide blast after a platform move can hurt deliverability. Segment your first sends to engaged contacts for two weeks.

7. Cancel HubSpot last. Keep it in read-only mode until your first two sends from ActiveCampaign go out clean, then downgrade or cancel inside HubSpot billing.

Post-Migration Verification

Send a test through every automation path, confirm tracking links resolve to your domain, and watch bounce rates on the first three sends. Check that your unsubscribe list carried over - re-emailing someone who opted out in HubSpot is a compliance problem, not just an annoyance.

FAQ

Q: How long does a HubSpot to ActiveCampaign migration take?

A: A solo operator with a simple setup can move in a weekend: exports and imports take an hour, rebuilding automations takes the bulk of it. Teams with 10+ workflows should budget a week or two.

Q: Will I lose my contact history and email opens?

A: Contact records and list membership carry over via CSV. Historical email-open data does not import - if that matters for segmentation, export it now and keep it in a spreadsheet.

Q: Does ActiveCampaign cost less than HubSpot at renewal?

A: Neither platform runs intro-then-renewal promos, so the entry rate is the renewal rate. At 1,000 contacts, ActiveCampaign Starter lists at $15/mo versus HubSpot's $20/mo Starter - and ActiveCampaign's price moves up in bands as your list grows, while HubSpot bills contact-tier overages (our published rate card shows $50 per 1,000 extra contacts on Professional).

Q: Can I keep my HubSpot CRM while moving email to ActiveCampaign?

A: Yes - ActiveCampaign's enhanced CRM is an add-on, but it integrates with plenty of external CRMs. Many teams move email first and revisit CRM later.
