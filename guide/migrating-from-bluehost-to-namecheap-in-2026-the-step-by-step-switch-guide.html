The Headline: cPanel to cPanel

Namecheap's shared hosting (Stellar, ~$1.98/mo intro billed yearly, renewing ~$4.48/mo per our comparison) runs cPanel - and so does Bluehost. That makes this one of the friendliest host-to-host moves: a full cPanel backup restores almost cleanly on the other side. Bluehost Basic is ~$2.95/mo on a 36-month intro, renewing ~$10.99/mo on our published rates - the first-year gap (~$23) widens to roughly $156/year at renewal. Confirm both at checkout.

Before You Switch: Prep Checklist

Full cPanel backup (files + databases + email), a second database-only dump as insurance, a list of DNS records, and your Bluehost renewal date. If your domain sits at Bluehost, decide whether to transfer it to Namecheap (EPP code needed, 5-7 days lead) or leave it and only point DNS.

The Step-by-Step Migration

1. Order Namecheap Stellar. Match the term to your plans - Stellar's intro is billed yearly. Stellar Plus (~$3.48/mo intro, ~$6.48 renewal) if you need unlimited sites; Stellar Business (~$5.48, ~$9.88) adds dedicated IP headroom.

2. Restore the cPanel backup. Upload your full backup via cPanel's Backup wizard on the Namecheap side, or use their migration ticket - Namecheap support restores cPanel backups for you.

3. Verify the site on staging. Use the temporary URL / hosts-file trick; click through pages, forms, and any checkout.

4. Reissue SSL. Namecheap auto-provisions Let's Encrypt (AutoSSL) - force it after restore so https works before cutover.

5. Lower TTLs. 300 seconds, a day before the flip.

6. Flip DNS. Point A records at Namecheap (or move nameservers). Propagation is minutes with low TTLs.

7. Cancel Bluehost. Confirm cancellation in writing via chat, note the final billing date, and keep your Bluehost backup archived locally for a month.

Post-Migration Verification

Check email flow (mailbox recreation + MX records), SSL coverage, and cron jobs (cPanel crons sometimes need re-pointing after restore). Run a speed test - Namecheap's entry tier is budget hardware, so watch TTFB if your site is heavy.

FAQ

Q: Is Namecheap's Stellar plan big enough for a small WordPress site?

A: For a typical small business site: yes - 20 GB SSD and 3 websites on Stellar. Heavy media sites should start at Stellar Plus.

Q: How does the two-year cost compare?

A: On our published rates: Namecheap Stellar ~$1.98/mo is about $47.52 over 24 months, renewing ~$4.48/mo. Bluehost Basic is ~$2.95/mo (~$70.80 over 24 months, 36-month intro), renewing ~$10.99/mo. Confirm current prices at checkout - promos change.

Q: Do I have to transfer my domain to Namecheap?

A: No. You can leave it at Bluehost and just point DNS at Namecheap hosting. Transferring consolidates billing and usually adds a year of registration.

Q: Will my email move automatically?

A: Mailboxes ride along in a full cPanel backup, but verify MX records and send test messages both ways before you cancel Bluehost.
