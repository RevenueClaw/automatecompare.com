Why the Renewal Date Matters Here

Our published rates: Bluehost Basic runs ~$2.95/mo on a 36-month intro term, then renews at ~$11.99/mo. Domain.com Basic starts ~$3.75/mo and renews at ~$10.99/mo. Both intro rates demand a long prepay, so the real decision is which renewal bill you can live with - and whether a 3x jump (Domain.com) beats a 4x jump (Bluehost) for your budget. Confirm current rates at checkout; hosting promos shift.

Before You Switch: Prep Checklist

Back up everything yourself - files plus a full database dump (phpMyAdmin export or a plugin like UpdraftPlus). Export your email forwarding rules, list every DNS record you've customized, and check your Bluehost renewal date so the move lands before the next charge. Domain transfers (if you bring the domain) need the registrar unlocked with the EPP/auth code in hand - start that 5-7 days early.

The Step-by-Step Migration

1. Order Domain.com hosting. Pick the term you actually want - the intro rate scales with the commitment length.

2. Move the files. Upload your backup to the new account (FTP/SFTP or the migration tool), recreate databases, and import the SQL dump.

3. Update wp-config (if WordPress). Point wp-config.php at the new database host, name, user, and password. Run a search-replace on the site URL if the temporary domain differs.

4. Stage and test. Domain.com gives a temporary URL or staging area - click through core pages, forms, and checkout before touching DNS.

5. Lower DNS TTL. A day before cutover, drop TTLs to 300 seconds in your current DNS so propagation takes minutes, not hours.

6. Flip DNS. Point the A record (or nameservers) at Domain.com. With low TTLs, the cutover is minutes.

7. Cancel Bluehost. Live-chat is the most reliable way - ask them to cancel auto-renewal and confirm any prorated refund. Do it after the site runs clean on the new host for a few days.

Post-Migration Verification

Watch uptime and email deliverability for a week (mail exchangers can be slow to re-learn new IPs), confirm SSL reissued on the new host, and keep your Bluehost backup on local storage for a month before deleting anything.

FAQ

Q: Can I migrate my domain to Domain.com too?

A: Yes - unlock it at Bluehost/FASTDOMAIN, grab the EPP code, and start the transfer at Domain.com. Transfers add a year of registration; don't transfer within 60 days of a registration or prior transfer (registry lock).

Q: How much will hosting cost over two years?

A: On our published rates: Bluehost Basic ~$2.95/mo means about $70.80 over 24 months (still inside the 36-month intro), renewing ~$11.99/mo after. Domain.com Basic is ~$3.75/mo (~$90.00 over 24 months), renewing ~$10.99/mo. Year two prices converge; the renewal rates decide years three onward.

Q: Will my site go down during the move?

A: Not if you stage first and lower TTLs - with the site fully tested on the new host, the DNS flip is minutes and old caches cover stragglers.

Q: Do I need to move my email hosting?

A: Mailboxes tied to Bluehost either move (recreate them on the new host and migrate mail) or stay put if you use third-party email. Recreate forwarding rules manually - they don't transfer.
