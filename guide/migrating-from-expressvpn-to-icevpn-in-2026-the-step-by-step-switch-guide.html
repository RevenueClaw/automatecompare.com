Why People Make the Switch

ExpressVPN verified checkout (November 22, 2026) shows its Basic 2-years-plus-4-months bundle at $78.12 for the first 28 months - about $2.79/mo effective - auto-renewing at $99.95/year. IceVPN's published 2-year rate is $59.76 flat (~$2.49/mo), renewing around $47.88/year. If saving roughly $40 a year matters to you, the switch math is real - just verify both prices at checkout, because VPN promos move constantly.

Before You Switch: Prep Checklist

List every device on your ExpressVPN plan (routers count - ExpressVPN's router app is one of its better features), note any split-tunneling or kill-switch settings you rely on, and check your ExpressVPN renewal date: cancel at least 24-48 hours before it renews. ExpressVPN renews at $99.95/year on the Basic bundle, and that charge lands automatically.

The Step-by-Step Migration

1. Pick your IceVPN plan. The 2-year plan (~$2.49/mo, $59.76) is the entry price worth having; the 1-year (~$3.99/mo) and monthly ($11.99) rates renew higher. IceVPN publishes 6-month (~$6.99/mo) and other terms too - confirm current pricing at checkout.

2. Install IceVPN everywhere first. Download IceVPN on your phone, laptop, and TV devices while ExpressVPN is still active, so you are never unprotected mid-switch.

3. Sign in and configure. Enable the kill switch, set your auto-connect rules (home Wi-Fi off, public Wi-Fi on), and replicate any split-tunneling you used on ExpressVPN.

4. Test before canceling. Run DNS-leak and WebRTC checks with IceVPN active, confirm streaming endpoints you care about, and note the speeds - budget VPNs vary more by server than ExpressVPN does.

5. Cancel ExpressVPN. Account dashboard > Subscription > cancel auto-renewal. ExpressVPN's 30-day money-back window covers recent subscriptions if you are inside it. Keep the app installed until your first IceVPN week goes smoothly, then remove it.

6. Handle the router. If ExpressVPN ran on your router, either flash IceVPN's config onto it (IceVPN supports WireGuard/OpenVPN router setups) or revert the router to stock and run device clients.

7. Mark your renewal date. IceVPN renews at its regular rate after the intro term - set a calendar reminder a week before so the renewal price never surprises you.

Post-Migration Verification

A week in: check leak tests on every network you use, confirm the kill switch actually drops traffic when the tunnel breaks, and verify no device silently fell back to ExpressVPN (leftover configs cause split-brain routing).

FAQ

Q: Can I get a refund from ExpressVPN when I switch?

A: ExpressVPN's 30-day money-back guarantee applies to new and recently renewed subscriptions - request it from your account dashboard within the window.

Q: Will switching VPNs affect my streaming or banking logins?

A: Expect one-time re-verifications: services that whitelist your old VPN IP will ask you to confirm again under the new provider. Banking apps generally just trigger a security check.

Q: Is IceVPN actually cheaper over two years?

A: IceVPN's published 2-year rate is $59.76 versus about $66.96 pro-rated over the same 24 months on ExpressVPN's verified $78.12/28-month Basic bundle - and the renewal rates (~$47.88/year vs $99.95/year) widen the gap. Confirm current prices at checkout; both vendors run frequent promos.

Q: Do I need to reinstall anything on my router?

A: If ExpressVPN ran at the router level, yes - either load IceVPN's OpenVPN/WireGuard config or revert the router and use device-level apps.
