Why Teams Move (and Why Some Don't)

Salesforce's flexibility is a feature until there's no admin left to flex it. Our comparison pegs a Salesforce-capable admin at $70,000-$95,000 and consultants at $150-$250/hour, with 6-12 week rollouts being normal. HubSpot Starter is $20/mo flat with no promo pricing, so the math for a small team is straightforward: you are trading configuration depth for not needing a dedicated admin.

Before You Switch: Prep Checklist

Document every Salesforce object you actually use (Leads, Contacts, Opportunities, custom objects), every automation (Process Builder/Flow rules), and every report leadership checks weekly. Decide early which custom objects must survive the move - HubSpot handles most B2B needs natively, but deep custom-object setups need the Operations Hub or a rethink.

The Step-by-Step Migration

1. Export Salesforce data. Data Export or Data Loader: Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Opportunities, plus Activities if you want history. CSVs all the way down.

2. Map Lead/Contact models to HubSpot. Salesforce Leads+Accounts usually collapse into HubSpot Companies+Contacts+Deals. Write the mapping down before import - it prevents duplicate companies.

3. Import into HubSpot. HubSpot's native Salesforce importer handles the heavy lifting if Salesforce is still live (Settings > Integrations > Salesforce). If you'd rather not connect both, import the CSVs directly.

4. Rebuild pipelines. Recreate your Salesforce opportunity stages as HubSpot deal stages, one for one. Resist renaming anything during migration - stage history reports get messy when names drift.

5. Rebuild automations. Salesforce Flows become HubSpot workflows. Rebuild the three highest-value sequences first, test with a sandbox deal, then port the rest. Sequence automation does not transfer - you rebuild it.

6. Migrate email sending. Connect your sending domain, verify DKIM/SPF, warm up before full sends.

7. Flip the cutover. Run both CRMs in parallel for two weeks with a single source-of-truth decision (HubSpot wins ties), freeze Salesforce to read-only, then downgrade your Salesforce licenses. Salesforce is list-priced per user with no intro promo, so every month you keep idle licenses is full price.

Post-Migration Verification

Reconcile deal totals against your final Salesforce export, confirm dashboards cover the reports leadership actually reads, and check activity sync (calls, emails, meetings) is populating timelines.

FAQ

Q: How much does Salesforce cost versus HubSpot for a small team?

A: Salesforce is list-priced per user with no intro discounts - pull the live rate at salesforce.com/pricing. As a labeled illustration only: at a $25/user/month list rate, one seat for 24 months would run about $600. HubSpot Starter is $20/mo flat (about $480 over two years per seat), published November 2026.

Q: How long does a Salesforce to HubSpot migration take?

A: Solo or small teams with standard objects: 2-4 weeks including parallel running. Teams with heavy custom objects and Flow libraries: 2-3 months, often with a consultant.

Q: Will we lose Salesforce history?

A: Records and closed activities export with the data. Salesforce-native reporting history and dashboards do not transfer - screenshot or rebuild what you need.

Q: Do we need an admin after moving to HubSpot?

A: Not in the Salesforce sense. HubSpot's Starter tier is built to be administered by the person using it; bigger Operations-Hub setups still benefit from an owner, but not a dedicated $70k+ admin.
