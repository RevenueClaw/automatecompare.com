Tally Review 2026: The Free Form Builder That Competes with Typeform

If you’ve ever tried to build a nice-looking form without spending a dime, you know the struggle. Google Forms is free but looks like it was designed in 2005. Typeform looks gorgeous but charges you after 100 responses. Jotform gets expensive fast. So when Tally showed up promising a truly free form builder with unlimited submissions and no branding, I had to put it through its paces.

I’ve been testing Tally for the last three weeks, building everything from a client intake form to a simple survey for my newsletter. Here’s my full Tally review—no fluff, just what works and what doesn’t.

What Is Tally? A Quick Overview

Tally is a web-based form builder that launched in 2021 and has been quietly growing among freelancers and small business owners. Its biggest selling point? A generous free plan that lets you create unlimited forms, collect unlimited submissions, and use all the core features—conditional logic, file uploads, payment integrations, even a calculator field—without paying a cent.

Where Typeform puts a hard cap at 10 responses per month on its free plan, Tally gives you everything. No response limits, no Tally branding on forms (unless you want it), and no time bombs pushing you to upgrade.

But is it too good to be true? Let’s dig into the details.

Detailed Feature Breakdown

1. Form Builder & Design

Tally’s editor is a clean, block-based interface. You add question types like short text, multiple choice, rating scales, file upload, date picker, and more. Each block can be customized with labels, placeholders, and help text.

The design options are decent but not as polished as Typeform. You can change fonts, colors, and background images, but there’s no full CSS control. For most small business forms—contact forms, order forms, surveys—it looks professional enough.

What Tally does well is speed. Forms load fast on mobile and desktop. No fancy animations (Typeform’s one-question-at-a-time style is missing here), but that’s actually a plus for users who just want to fill out a form quickly.

2. Conditional Logic

This is where Tally shines. You can set up show/hide rules, skip pages, and even calculate values based on previous answers. For example, a pricing calculator form that adds up selected services and displays a total. All of this is available on the free plan.

Logic rules are visual: you pick a question, set a condition (equals, contains, greater than, etc.), and choose what happens. It’s straightforward and works without lag.

3. Templates & Pre-built Forms

Tally has a template gallery with about 50+ templates sorted by use case: lead generation, event registration, order forms, feedback, etc. They’re a good starting point, but you’ll likely want to tweak them. The templates are clean and modern, though some feel a bit generic.

4. Integrations

Tally connects with over 1,000 apps via Zapier, Make (formerly Integromat), and its native integrations. Native ones include:

  • Google Sheets (two-way sync)
  • Slack
  • Notion
  • Airtable
  • Mailchimp
  • Stripe / PayPal (for payments)

The Google Sheets integration is a lifesaver. Every submission instantly appears in a spreadsheet, no coding needed. On the free plan, you get one integration at a time. Paid plans unlock unlimited integrations.

5. Payments & File Uploads

You can collect payments through Stripe or PayPal directly inside a form. Tally doesn’t take a cut—only the payment processor’s fees apply. File uploads are supported with a 10 MB limit per file on the free plan (100 MB on paid). Good for client intake forms that need PDFs or images.

6. Data Export & Analytics

Tally provides a basic dashboard with submission counts, completion rates, and a list of all entries. You can export data as CSV or PDF. No advanced analytics like Typeform’s heatmaps, but for most needs it’s fine.

7. Branding & Custom Domain

Free forms can be branded with your own logo and colors, but you cannot remove the “Powered by Tally” footer unless you upgrade to the Pro plan ($29/month). The footer is small and unobtrusive, but if you want a fully white-label experience, you’ll need to pay.

Pros and Cons

✅ What I Liked

  • Truly free for unlimited use. No hidden caps. You can run a business with the free plan.
  • Easy conditional logic. Setting up show/hide rules and calculators takes minutes.
  • Google Sheets integration works out of the box. Perfect for automating data entry.
  • Fast loading forms. No heavy JavaScript animations.
  • Payment collection without extra fees. Just Stripe/PayPal fees.
  • Responsive customer support. Even free users get email support (usually within 24 hours).

❌ What Could Be Better

  • Design flexibility is limited. You can’t change the layout as freely as Typeform. For example, you can’t put two fields side by side.
  • No pre-filled URL parameters on the free plan (Pro feature). This is a pain for sending personalized form links.
  • The “Powered by Tally” footer is always present on free forms. It’s small, but some clients might notice.
  • No mobile app. You manage forms only through the web dashboard.
  • Analytics are basic. No drop-off tracking or A/B testing.

Pricing Breakdown

Tally’s pricing is simple:

There’s also a 14-day free trial of Pro if you want to test the extra features.

Compare that to Typeform: $25/month for 100 responses, or $59/month for unlimited. Tally’s Pro at $29 gives you unlimited submissions and no branding. That’s a steal.

Who Should Use Tally?

Tally is perfect for:

  • Freelancers and solopreneurs who need a professional-looking form without a monthly bill.
  • Small businesses collecting leads, orders, or client info with moderate design needs.
  • Anyone who wants to automate workflows with Google Sheets, Notion, or Slack.
  • Budget-conscious teams that don’t need advanced analytics or custom CSS.

Tally might not be right for:

  • Large enterprises that need white-labeling and dedicated support.
  • Marketers running high-volume surveys with complex branching and A/B testing.
  • Users who need a fully customizable design (e.g., matching exact brand guidelines).

Final Verdict

After three weeks of heavy use, I can confidently say Tally is the best free form builder for small businesses right now. It delivers everything Typeform promises at a fraction of the cost—and the free plan is actually usable.

If you’re a freelancer or a small team just starting out, start with Tally’s free plan. You won’t hit a wall after a few submissions. When you outgrow it, the Pro plan at $29 is still cheaper than Typeform’s basic plan for unlimited responses.

My recommendation: Check Tally -> and build your first form today. It’s rare to find a tool that gives this much value for free.

FAQ

1. Is Tally really free?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited forms, unlimited submissions, conditional logic, file uploads, and payment integrations. The only limitation is a small “Powered by Tally” footer and a cap of one active integration at a time.

2. How does Tally compare to Typeform?

Tally is more affordable and has a much better free plan. Typeform offers a more polished, conversational design and better analytics, but you pay significantly more for the same functionality.

3. Can I remove the Tally branding?

Only on the Pro plan ($29/month). The free plan shows a small footer that says “Powered by Tally.”

4. Does Tally support payments?

Yes. You can collect payments via Stripe or PayPal. No additional fee from Tally—only the payment processor’s standard charges apply.

5. Can I embed Tally forms on my website?

Absolutely. Tally provides an embed code (iframe or popup) that works on any site. You can also share a direct link.

6. What happens if I exceed the free plan limits?

There are no limits on submissions or forms. The only limit is integrations (one at a time). If you need more integrations, you’ll need Pro.

7. Is Tally secure?

Tally uses SSL encryption, follows GDPR guidelines, and stores data on secure servers. They also offer a privacy policy and data processing agreement for businesses.
